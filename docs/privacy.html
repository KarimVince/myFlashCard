Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2025 Last updated: September 14, 2025 Version 1.0

myFlashCard does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy describes how WillYGO Incorporation ("we", "our", or "us") handles information in connection with the myFlashCard mobile application for Android ("the app").

By using the app you confirm that you have read and understood this policy.

Information we collect

We collect no personal information of any kind. Specifically:

Account or identity data
Not collected. The app requires no account, login, or registration.
Device identifiers
Not collected. We do not read your device ID, advertising ID, or any hardware identifier.
Location data
Not collected. The app does not request or use location permissions.
Usage analytics
Not collected. There is no analytics SDK, crash reporter, or telemetry in the app.
Flashcard content
JSON deck files you load are read locally on your device and never sent anywhere.
Network requests
The app makes no network requests. It operates fully offline.

How your data is stored

Flashcard decks are JSON files that you select from your own device storage. The app reads them into memory to display your cards. No data is written to external storage or transmitted over the network.

When you close the app, no residual data is retained beyond what Android's standard app lifecycle manages on your device.

Third-party services

The app uses no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics services, or cloud platforms. There is nothing to share with any third party because no data is collected in the first place.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because we collect no data at all, the app is safe for users of any age in this respect.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top. We will notify users of material changes through an in-app notice or an updated listing on Google Play. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
